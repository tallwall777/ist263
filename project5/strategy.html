Strategy Guide

Warzone isn't just about aim or camping, it's about decisions. The weapons you choose, the way you move, and how you communicate with your team all matter. This page breaks down key strategies that can help you survive longer, win more fights, and turn close games into wins.

Meta Loadouts and Weapon Choices

In Warzone, a loadout is the custom weapon and perk setup you use once you buy or receive a Loadout Drop. Choosing the right loadout can make a huge difference in gunfights.

When building a loadout, think about:

  • Primary Weapon:
    • Assault rifles and LMGs for mid-range fights
    • SMGs for close-range, face-paced combat
    • Sniper rifles or marksman rifles for long-range engagements

  • Secondary Weapon:
    • SMGs or shotgun if your primary is long-range
    • Pistol or launcher if you need utility

  • Attachments: Focus on recoil control, damage range, and aim down sight speed. A weapon that feels comfortable and consistent is better than one that's “meta” but hard for you to control.

  • Perks and Equipment: Use perks that fit your playstyle, whether that's staying off radar, carrying more equipment, or swapping weapons faster. Tactical and lethal equipment (like stuns, smokes, and grenades) can help you win fights before you even fire a shot.

Movement Tips

Good movement can be the difference between getting deleted instantly or escaping with one HP. Here are some key movement strategies to use:

  • Use Cover: Always move from cover to cover, walls, rocks, buildings, and vehicles. Avoid running in open areas for long periods.

  • Slide and Dive: Sliding and diving can help you break enemy aim, but spamming movement without thinking can make you predicatable. Use slides to get behind cover or to close gaps quickly.

  • Mantle and Reposition: Climbing onto rooftops or high ground gives you bettter sightlines and angles. High ground often gives you an advantage in fights.

  • Don't Stand Still: When shooting, try to strafe or make small movements like jumping or laying down as you shoot. Standing still makes you an easy target.

Rotations and Positioning

Rotations are how you move around the map as the gas circle closes. Smart rotations can keep you alive even if you're not winning every fight.

Ask Yourself:

  • Where is the next circle? Try to move early instead of waiting until the gas is right behind you. Early rotations give you time to find good positions. Early rotations also allow you to hold of enemies in the storm as they try to move into the new zone.

  • Can I avoid crowded areas? Hot spots like big buildings or popular Buy Stations can be risky. Sometimes it's better to rotate around the edges of the circle depending on how crowded an area may be.

  • Do I have a vehicle or utility? Vehicles can help you rotate quickly, but they also make noise and draw attention. Use them carefully.

Team Tactics and Communication

Warzone is at its best when played with a squad. Good communication can turn a group of average players into a strong team

  • Call out enemy positions: Use clear directions like “on the roof,” “behind the truck,” or “pushing left.” Ping enemies and locations on the map.

  • Share resources: Drop extra plates, ammo, and cash for teammates. A full plated team is much stronger than one player hoarding everything.

  • Plan pushes and retreats: Decide together whether you're taking a fight or backing off. If one teammate pushes alone, they'll often get eliminated quickly.

  • Revive and support: Prioritize reviving teammates when it's safe. A full squad has more guns, more angles, and more chances to win.
