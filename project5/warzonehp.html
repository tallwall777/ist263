Welcome to Call of Duty: Warzone

Call of Duty: Warzone is a free to play battle royale and extraction style shooter where squads drop into large maps, fight for survival, and race to be the last team standing. Warzone mixes fast paced Call of Duty gameplay with large scaled tactical decision making, making it one of the most competitive shooting games available today.

What is Warzone?

Warzone drops you and your squad into a massive map with dozens of other players. You start with basic weapons and must quickly loot buildings, buy gear, and win gunfights to stay alive. As the match goes on, a deadly gas circle closes in, forcing players into tighter areas and more intense fights.

Core Gameplay Mechanics

Warzone's gameplay is built around a few key mechanics:

  • Movement: Players can sprint, slide, dive, wall jump, mantle over obstacles, and use tactical equipment to reposition. Good movement helps you dodge bullets, break enemy lines of sight, and escape bad situations.

  • Combat: Gunfights are fast and punishing. Weapons have different recoil patterns, damage ranges, and attachments. Learning how each weapon feels and how to control recoil is a big part of improving in Warzone.

  • Looting and Economy: You collect cash, weapons, armor plates, and equipment from the ground, supply boxes, and defeated enemies. Cash can be spent at Buy Stations to purchase loadouts, self revives, UAVs, and other powerful items that can swing a match in your favor.

Maps and Modes

Warzone features multiple large scale maps and different modes that change how you play:

  • Battle Royale: The classic mode where teams drop in, loot, and fight until only one squad remains. The gas circle shrinks over time, forcing constant movement and decision making. The Gulag is an element of this mode. The Gulag is a small arena where you fight another player in a 1v1. If you win, you redeploy into the match. The Gulag adds a second chance mechanic that keeps games intense and gives players hope even after losing early fights.

  • Resurgnece: A faster paced mode on smaller maps where players can respawn as long as at least one teammate is alive. This mode rewards aggressive play and constant fighting.

  • Limited-Time Modes (LTMs): Warzone frequently rotates special modes, such as snipers only, mini royale, or unique event modes. These keep the game fresh and give players new challenges.
